Kirkland Announces Planned January Arrival of 40 Lawyers From Weil

A large team move creates a transition period before it creates a new working arrangement.

United States; Canadian law-firm business comparison

A large team move creates a transition period before it creates a new working arrangement.

Kirkland & Ellis announced on November 6 that 40 lawyers from Weil, Gotshal & Manges will join the firm in January 2027. Its announcement names private equity and M&A partners Ramona Nee, Jakub Wronski, Emily Willey and Michael Messina, and debt finance partners Benton Lewis and Paul Overmyer as team leaders.

The timing matters: the announcement concerns a future arrival for the entire group, rather than confirmation that all 40 lawyers have already joined.

For clients, a team announcement invites practical questions. Who remains responsible for active matters before the move? What conflicts checks will be required? How will documents, permissions and communications be handled if a client chooses to instruct the receiving firm?

For law firms, coordinated recruitment also creates a staffing question. Moving transactional and financing experience together may support continuity, but continuity must be organized matter by matter.

The announcement itself does not establish that particular clients or files will transfer.

Nor does this recruitment development resolve the separately held claims about Weil’s dissolution process or the sale of institutional assets.

A team can announce its destination before any client has decided to follow.

Sources: Kirkland’s announcement; Reuters report.

Scope: This package clears the independently confirmed recruitment announcement only. All earlier Weil holds remain.
